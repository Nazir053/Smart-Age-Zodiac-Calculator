# Privacy Policy — Smart Age & Zodiac Calculator

**Effective Date:** November 9, 2026  
**Application Name:** Smart Age & Zodiac Calculator  
**Package Name:** `com.nazir.smartagecalculator`

## 1. Overview & Local-First Privacy
**Smart Age & Zodiac Calculator** is designed as an offline-first personal utility. We do not require user accounts, registration, or cloud sign-in.

## 2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
- **Dates of Birth & Calculation History:** Any date of birth you calculate or pin as a quick profile is stored strictly locally on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore.
- **User Preferences:** Theme mode (Light/Dark/System), accent color palette, language preference (English/Bengali), haptic feedback, and animation toggles are saved locally on your device.
- **No Server Transmission:** Your date of birth and profile labels are never uploaded to any external database or shared with advertising networks.

## 3. Advertisements (Google AdMob)
- The application integrates Google AdMob for banner, frequency-capped interstitial, and optional rewarded advertisements.
- Birth date calculations are never used for ad targeting or profiling.

## 4. Data Deletion & User Control
You have complete control over your locally stored data. At any time, you can:
- Delete individual saved dates in the **History** tab.
- Tap **Clear All History** in the **History** or **Settings** screen to permanently wipe all locally stored calculations and saved birth dates.
